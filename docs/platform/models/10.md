# Phase 10 — Membership, Payments & Access Control

This is the phase where your platform becomes a **business**, not just an LMS.

This phase controls **who can access what**.

Everything eventually passes through the Access Control Engine.

```text
Visitor

↓

Login

↓

Enrollment?

↓

Purchase?

↓

Membership?

↓

Access Granted
```

> **Important**
>
> Since you're building locally, **don't integrate Razorpay/Stripe now**.
>
> Create a Payment Provider abstraction so later you can plug in Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal without changing business logic.

# Validation Rules

### Membership

* Name required
* Price ≥ 0
* Billing cycle required

### Purchase

* User cannot purchase the same course twice unless refunded
* Free courses bypass payment

### Coupon

* Must not be expired
* Must not exceed max usage
* Must belong to the same institution

### Payment

* Amount must equal calculated order total
* Payment record created before enrollment

# Business Rules

* Every institution manages its own membership plans.
* A membership plan can unlock multiple courses.
* Course purchases and memberships are independent; a learner may have either or both.
* Free courses bypass the payment flow but still create an enrollment record.
* Coupons are institution-specific and validated before payment.
* Payment providers must be abstracted behind a service interface (`PaymentProvider`) so switching from dummy payments to Razorpay or Stripe requires minimal changes.
* **Never** check access directly in controllers. All access decisions must go through a centralized `AccessService`.
* Payment records are immutable except for status transitions (e.g., `PENDING → SUCCESS`).
