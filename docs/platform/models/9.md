* Due date required

## Submission

* Student must be enrolled
* Assignment must be published
* Due date must not be expired unless late submission is enabled

# Business Rules

* Every lesson can contain multiple quizzes and assignments.
* Students must have an active enrollment to attempt assessments.
* Quiz attempts respect the configured attempt limit.
* Objective questions (MCQ, Multiple Choice, True/False) are graded automatically.
* Subjective questions (Short Answer) require instructor evaluation or AI-assisted grading in a future phase.
* Assignment submissions reference uploaded files via the Storage Service (`file_id`).
* Students can only view results if `show_result` is enabled or after manual grading.
* Grades contribute to learner analytics and certificate eligibility in later phases.
* Deleting a quiz or assignment performs a soft delete to preserve historical attempt and submission records.
