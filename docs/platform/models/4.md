# Phase 4 — Institution Members & Team Management

This phase transforms your platform from a **single-owner academy** into a **real coaching institution**.

A coaching institute may have:

* Owner
* Co-owner
* Instructor
* Teaching Assistant
* Content Team
* Video Editor
* Support Team
* Marketing Team
* Finance Team

Initially, this phase manages **team members only**. Role definitions and permissions will be expanded in the next phase.

---

# Goal

Allow an institution owner to:

* Invite members
* Accept invitations
* Remove members
* Suspend members
* View activity
* Manage member status

# Business Rules

* Only the institution **Owner** can invite members in this phase.
* Invitations are sent by **email** and expire after **7 days**.
* A user can belong to multiple institutions.
* An institution can have unlimited members (plan limits can be added later).
* Accepting an invitation automatically creates an `institution_member` record.
* Suspended members cannot access institution resources but remain in the database.
* Removing a member does not delete their user account.
* Every invitation generates an audit log (to be implemented in a later Audit phase).
* Permissions are **not** enforced yet; members are simply associated with the institution. The next phase introduces **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**.
