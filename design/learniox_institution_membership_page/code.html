Frequently Asked Questions

How does billing work?

Billing is processed monthly on the date you signed up. You will receive an email receipt for each transaction. We accept all major credit cards and institutional billing options.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your membership at any time from your account settings. Your access will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

What happens to my certificates if I cancel membership?

Any certificates you have earned and completed while your membership was active are yours to keep forever. They will remain verifiable on our platform regardless of your active membership status.
