About Instructor

Dr. Jenkins holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from MIT, specializing in probabilistic graphical models. Her pedagogical approach emphasizes rigorous mathematical foundations paired immediately with practical Python implementation.

Before entering academia full-time, she spent a decade as a Lead Data Scientist at major tech conglomerates, architecting recommendation systems that operate at a global scale. She brings this enterprise-level pragmatism into every lecture.
